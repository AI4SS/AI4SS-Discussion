# AI4SS Discussion

## 1. About the Discussion Group

AI4SS Discussion is an open discussion group focused on AI + **social cognition**. The group was initiated by the survey [*Social Intelligence Modeling: A Comprehensive Survey from Social Perception to Social Simulation*](https://doi.org/10.13140/RG.2.2.21157.87528) ([Project Page](https://sait-crypto.github.io/Awesome-Social-Intelligence-Modeling-System/)), which presents Social Intelligence Modeling (SIM) as a unified perspective for studying how AI perceives, understands, generates, and simulates human and collective behavior in digital social systems.

We welcome researchers, students, and practitioners interested in AI4SS to join the discussion, exchange ideas, and explore new research directions together.
